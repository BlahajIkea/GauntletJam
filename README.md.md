# Why I chose Raylib:

I chose Raylib due to its simplicity, being one of the easiest libraries to work with in C++. For a beginner, as this Gauntlet was my very first time In C++ and in OOP in general,
it was very good at applying my knowledge I had gained throughout the Gauntlet, and using it to make a fun game in less than 5 Days.

The large amount sources and the Raylib.com documents online
explained the usage of certain functions well, and how to put certain features into your game to make it more fun.

Also, due to the amount of work put into the library by Ray and his development team.
Raylib is very understandable and easy to pick up and make games in, when deciding not to use a tradtional game engine. 

I have done gamejams in the past and have experence in Unity (like 3 years ago), and currently doing Godot working on a mini project with a friend of mine.
I did not expect to have this much fun working on a game outside of a traditional game engine, as my own experence is just working with game engines only.
